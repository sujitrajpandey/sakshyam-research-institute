Lila Nath Sharma is a Senior Researcher at Sakshyam Research Institute specializing in biodiversity and forest-ecology. With a PhD in vegetation ecology from the University of Bergen, he combines scientific research with field based conservation and policy engagement. His work focuses on understanding ecosystems and translating ecological knowledge into practice approaches for sustainable forest and biodiversity management practices in Nepal.
