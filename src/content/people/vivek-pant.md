Dr. Vivek Pant completed MD in clinical chemistry from Tribhuvan University (Institute of Medicine) in 2018. He currently serves as a consultant and research director at the department of clinical chemistry in Samyak Diagnostic which is the first accredited clinical laboratory in Nepal. He is a certified internal auditor for assessing management and technical competencies in clinical laboratories.

Dr. Pant has authored more than 45 scientific articles and over a dozen presentations in various national and international conferences of clinical chemistry and Laboratory Medicine. Vivek is a senior researcher at Sakshyam.
