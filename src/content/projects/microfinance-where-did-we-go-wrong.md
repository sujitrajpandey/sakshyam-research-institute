---
title: "Microfinance: Where did we go wrong?"
summary: "Understanding how the microfinance sector in Nepal lost its way and exploring policy decisions to guide it back to empowering marginalized people."
---
The last couple of years have seen the microfinance sector vilified as profiteers rather than vehicles of economic empowerment for people at the bottom of the pyramid[cite: 1]. Under this project, we are trying to understand how the microfinance sector in Nepal lost its way[cite: 1]. We are focusing particularly on the policy decisions taken by the government and NRB, and their effect on how decision making at microfinance institutions[cite: 1]. The findings of the study will help NRB and the government take policy decisions that will guide the sector towards its original mission of economically empowering the marginalized people[cite: 1].
