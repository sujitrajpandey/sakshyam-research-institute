The last few years have seen the microfinance sector vilified as profiteers rather than vehicles of economic empowerment for people at the bottom of the pyramid. Under this project, we are trying to understand how the microfinance sector in Nepal lost its way. We are focusing particularly on the policy decisions taken by the government and NRB, and their effect on how decision making at microfinance institutions. The findings of the study will help NRB and the government take policy decisions that will guide the sector towards its original mission of economically empowering the marginalized people.
