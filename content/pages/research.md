A good research design is not defined by one method. We select methods based on the question, data, identification challenge, context, and decision the evidence is intended to support.

Depending on the project, our work can include literature reviews, theory of change development, survey design, administrative data analysis, interviews, focus groups, case studies, econometric analysis, quasi-experimental designs, experiments, and mixed-method research.
