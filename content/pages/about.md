## Our purpose

Sakshyam seeks to strengthen the quality of decisions made by policymakers, development practitioners, organizations, and entrepreneurs by producing credible, relevant, and accessible evidence.

Our work is rooted in Nepal while engaging with ideas, methods, and evidence from across the world. We are interested not only in describing problems, but in understanding mechanisms, identifying constraints, evaluating interventions, and translating findings into practical choices.

## How we work

- **Question first.** We begin with the decision or problem that the research needs to inform.
- **Method follows the question.** We combine quantitative, qualitative, experimental, quasi-experimental, and mixed-method approaches where appropriate.
- **Context matters.** Research is interpreted in light of Nepal's economic, institutional, social, and geographic realities.
- **Knowledge should travel.** Findings are translated into reports, briefs, articles, datasets, presentations, and other accessible formats.
